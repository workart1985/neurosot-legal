User Data Deletion Instructions — NeuroSot

Last updated: September 13, 2026

You may request deletion of data associated with your use of the NeuroSot application.

How to request deletion

  1. Disconnect or remove NeuroSot from the connected Instagram/Meta account, where applicable.
  2. Send a deletion request to the NeuroSot application operator using the contact method provided with the application. Identify the Instagram account for which deletion is requested.
  3. After the request is verified, data stored by NeuroSot that is associated with that account and is not required to be retained by law will be deleted within a reasonable period.

What may be deleted

This may include stored Instagram account metadata, media-related metadata, comments or message data obtained through the API, analytics data, and stored access credentials associated with the disconnected account, to the extent such information is stored by NeuroSot.

Platform data

Deleting data from NeuroSot does not delete information held independently by Instagram or Meta. Users should use Meta's own account and privacy controls for data maintained by those services.
