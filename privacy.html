Privacy Policy — NeuroSot

Last updated: September 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the NeuroSot application processes information when connected to Instagram Professional accounts through Meta's Instagram API.

Information we process

Depending on the permissions granted by the account owner, NeuroSot may process Instagram account and profile information, media and publishing information, comments and replies, messages and conversation information, and account or media insights and analytics made available through the Instagram API.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide the application's Instagram management and automation features, including publishing content, viewing and managing comments, processing account messages, and displaying or analyzing account and content performance.

Sharing of information

NeuroSot does not sell personal information. Information obtained through Meta APIs is not shared with third parties except service providers necessary to operate the application, where applicable, or when required by law.

Data storage and security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect credentials and data processed by the application. Access tokens and other credentials are intended to be stored securely and are not intentionally exposed publicly.

Data retention

Data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the application's functionality or meet legal requirements. Data associated with a disconnected account may be deleted when it is no longer required.

User choices and deletion

Users may disconnect the application from their Instagram/Meta account and may request deletion of data associated with their use of NeuroSot. See the User Data Deletion Instructions.

Third-party platforms

NeuroSot uses Meta's Instagram API. Use of Instagram and Meta services is also subject to the terms and privacy policies provided by Meta.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the application changes. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the NeuroSot application operator through the contact method provided with the application.
